The gallery

In Hindu cosmogony, Agni is the sacrificial fire, the force that transforms matter into energy; in Japanese tradition, Ka embodies passion, movement, and will. Blue belongs to this dual lineage. On his incandescent promontory, the bucket he carries is no mere container: it is the Promethean gesture by which humanity wrenched from the volcano its most burning secret. Behind him, the eruption rumbles — relic of an era when Agni reigned unchallenged. Today, Blue carries it. Domesticated. Tamed. Mundane. The force that once devoured civilisations now serves to boil water for tea.

The artist

He stole fire from a volcano. For tea.

C. S.
