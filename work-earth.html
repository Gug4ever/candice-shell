The gallery

Prithvi is the Earth goddess, the support of all existence; Chi, in the Godai, embodies solidity, resistance, that which endures. Helmet fixed on skull, lamp raised like a relic, Red advances through the cave with the quiet assurance of the discoverer. But the statue that looms over him, carved by forgotten hands, reminds him of the obvious: he is not the first. Even in the very bowels of Prithvi, humanity has preceded him. The work interrogates this vertiginous paradox inherent to the Earth element — those who believe they explore most often only rediscover. The Earth keeps everything. The Earth remembers.
