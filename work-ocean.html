The gallery

Something is there, in the depths. Orange knows it. His flippers, his scuba tank, all his equipment are no help against what he cannot name. Shark? Shadow? The mysteries of the ocean cannot be catalogued. The work explores that borderline where preparation gives way to dread. You can be perfectly equipped and still be afraid.

The artist

All the gear in the world, and still that face.
