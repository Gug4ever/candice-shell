The gallery

He doesn't doubt. That's what makes him fascinating — and slightly insufferable. Red in the city moves through the skyscrapers with the quiet certainty of someone who feels at home everywhere: perfect bowler hat, briefcase in hand, small city shoes clicking on the asphalt. The work interrogates confidence as a social construction. Or perhaps just as a temperament.

The artist

He thinks he's at home everywhere. He's wrong, with great confidence.

C. S.
