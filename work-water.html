The gallery

Jala, in Vedic thought, is the primordial matrix, the cosmic amniotic fluid; Sui, in the Japanese Godai, designates that which flows, adapts, and takes the form of its vessel. The sole female figure in the M&M pantheon — troubling coincidence, or symbolic inevitability? — Green rises at the heart of an aquatic sanctuary, arms open in welcome. Water, source of all life, envelops her. The white horse in the background speaks of ancestral purity; its saddle speaks of conquest. The work oscillates between celebration and domestication, between primordial fluidity and human mastery. The rainbow is not décor: it is the covenant, in both the biblical and Vedic sense.
